Welcome to EK-Agroprocessing Dynasty

EK-Agroprocessing Dynasty is an agribusiness company that specializes in cocoa procurements, poultry, agrodealing,fishery, cocoa nursery, and other agro services. Our mission is to become a leading player in the agricultural sector by providing high-quality products and services, while promoting sustainable agricultural practices.

Our Services

Cocoa Procurements

Ek Agro processng Dynasty procures high quality cocoa beans from smallholder farmers and supplies them to food processors and chocolate manufacturers.

Poultry

The Company operates a poultry farm, producing both broilers and layers.

Agrodealing

Ek Agro processing Dynasty provides agrodealing services to farmers and agricultural businesses, including input supply, equipment rental, and technical advisory services.

Fishery

The company runs a fishery, producing fish for local consumption and export.

Cocoa Nursery

Ek Agro processng Dynasty has established a cocoa nursery that produces high high-quality cocoa seedlings for smallholder farmers.

Other Agro Services

The company offers various agricultural services, including soil testing, fertilizer application, and pest control.
